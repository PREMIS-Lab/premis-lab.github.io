About the Laboratory

The PREMIS lab is dedicated to the development and clinical translation of computational tools for medical imaging. We operate at the intersection of computer science, radiology and oncology.

Three threads run through that work: radiology, where we build quantitative image analysis across MRI and CT; computational pathology, applying deep learning to whole-slide images to characterize tumor and immune phenotypes; and therapeutic discovery, where AI systems link imaging with omics to uncover new targets.

Across all three, we extract quantitative biomarkers from standard-of-care imaging to predict patient outcomes, monitor treatment response and stratify patients for clinical trials. By integrating these imaging features with genomic data, we aim to advance the field of precision medicine.

Principal Investigators

Leading multidisciplinary teams to solve the hardest problems in cancer imaging.

Albert E. Kim, MD

Co-Director

Instructor in Neurology, Harvard Medical School · MGH Cancer Center

Dr. Kim is a physician-scientist and neuro-oncologist at the Mass General Cancer Center and the MGH Brain Tumor Research Center. He combines omics-based techniques (single-cell genomics, transcriptomics, cell-free DNA) with medical imaging and machine learning to define and target the genomic and metabolic vulnerabilities of central nervous system metastases, and to uncover the mechanisms behind therapeutic resistance.

MD, Medical College of Georgia · Neurology residency, Washington University · Fellowship, MGH and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute · Damon Runyon William G. Kaelin, Jr., MD Physician-Scientist Award.

Christopher P. Bridge, DPhil

Co-Director

Assistant Professor of Radiology, Harvard Medical School

Dr. Bridge works on machine learning for cancer imaging biomarkers across radiology, pathology and ophthalmology, and on the infrastructure required to move those models into clinical practice. He is the lead developer of highdicom and a contributor to the NCI Imaging Data Commons, with a long-standing focus on DICOM standards and interoperability for AI.

MEng, University of Cambridge · DPhil in Biomedical Engineering, University of Oxford · Formerly Director of Machine Learning at Mass General Brigham AI (2017–2022).
